01 Information We Collect

When you visit our website, submit a project request, or contact us directly, we may collect the following personal and project details:

  • Full Name — to address you and prepare formal proposals.
  • Email Address — for communication, sharing estimates, and delivering updates.
  • Phone Number / WhatsApp — for rapid communication and milestone calls.
  • Business or Brand Name — to tailor design concepts to your brand identity.
  • Project Requirements — features, scope, pages, and technical specifications.
  • Budget Details — to recommend suitable technical solutions within your parameters.
  • Messages & Form Enquiries — any details provided via our online contact interfaces.
  • Files & Media — logos, content, brand guides, images, or documents you voluntarily share.

02 How We Use Your Information

We treat client data with utmost care and use your information exclusively for legitimate business and project delivery purposes:

  • To respond promptly to your enquiries and project consultation requests.
  • To analyze and thoroughly understand your project requirements.
  • To provide professional website design and custom WordPress development services.
  • To prepare transparent quotations, formal proposals, milestones, and invoices.
  • To communicate ongoing project progress, staging previews, and status updates.
  • To deliver final completed website files, documentation, and handover assets.
  • To continually improve our service quality, speed, and client satisfaction.

03 Data Sharing & Non-Disclosure

Zero Data Sale Guarantee
We strictly do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or business information to third-party advertisers or brokers under any circumstance.

We may share limited project-relevant data only when strictly required for project completion — such as configuring domain registrars, setting up web hosting providers, integrating payment gateways, or connecting third-party developer APIs requested by you — and only to the minimal extent necessary.

04 Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Online Security Notice
While we maintain strong security protocols (including SSL encryption and isolated project environments), please be aware that no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% invulnerable. We encourage safe credential exchange practices.

05 Cookies and Tracking

Our website may utilize lightweight essential cookies and standard analytics tools to monitor website performance, understand user navigation patterns, and enhance your browsing experience.

You have the freedom to configure your browser to decline cookies or alert you when cookies are sent. Essential features of our site will continue functioning smoothly even with cookies disabled.

06 Third-Party Links & Services

Our website may contain hyperlinks leading to external websites, such as client portfolio showcases, social media profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram), web hosting companies, or third-party tools.

Please note that FYNFO is not responsible for the privacy practices, policies, or content of those external websites. We advise reviewing their respective privacy policies upon navigating away.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate records, or request complete deletion of your details from our records once active projects are concluded.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at hello.fynfo@gmail.com with your request, and we will fulfill it promptly.
